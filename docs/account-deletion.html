Voxera — Account deletion request

App name on Google Play: Voxera · Developer: VOICElab Limited

This page explains how users of the Voxera mobile application (developer: VOICElab Limited) can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

Quick request: email info@avoice.tech or voice.lab.ltd@gmail.com with subject Voxera account deletion.

Step-by-step: how to request account deletion

  1. Open your email app.
  2. Write to info@avoice.tech or voice.lab.ltd@gmail.com.
  3. Use the subject line: Voxera account deletion.
  4. In the message body include:
    • the email address used for Voxera registration or Google Sign-In;
    • the phrase: “Please delete my Voxera account and related data.”
  5. We will confirm the request and complete deletion within 30 days of verifying your identity (matching the email on the account).

What data will be deleted

  • Firebase Authentication account (email/password and/or Google Sign-In).
  • Profile and account-related data stored by us for that user.
  • Voice recordings and analysis results linked to your account on our servers (if any are retained server-side).

What data may be retained

  • Anonymized or aggregated analytics that cannot reasonably identify you.
  • Data we are legally required to keep (for example tax or accounting records), only if applicable, and only for the period required by law.
  • Backups may temporarily contain deleted data until the next backup rotation (typically up to 30 days).

Local data on your device

Uninstalling Voxera removes local app data on the device. Account deletion via this request removes the cloud/account side as described above.
